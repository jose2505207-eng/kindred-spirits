Deleting your account

Kindred Spirits · how to delete your account and what happens to your data when you do.

In the app

  1. Open Kindred Spirits and sign in.
  2. Go to the About you tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and choose Delete account.
  4. Confirm. The account and its data go immediately.

There is no waiting period and no reactivation. Signing up again with the same email address creates a new, empty account.

Without the app

If you cannot sign in — a lost password, a lost device, or an uninstalled app — email us from the address on the account and ask for it to be deleted. Say that you want the account deleted; you do not need to give a reason.

privacy@kindred-spirits.example

We answer within 30 days and normally much sooner. We may have to ask you to confirm from the account's own email address, so that nobody can delete somebody else's account.

Before this page goes live. The address above is a placeholder on the reserved .example domain and does not receive mail. It has to be replaced with a real, monitored mailbox before the app is submitted to either store.

What is deleted

Your account and sign-inDeleted
Name, birthdate, bio, visibilityDeleted
Photos and your bowtie imageDeleted from storage
Connections and matchesDeleted
Messages you sent, and your conversationsDeleted
Blocks you madeDeleted
Reports you filedDeleted

Your reading is never stored, so there is nothing to delete: cards are worked out from your birthdate on your own device each time, and the birthdate goes with the account.

What is kept

If somebody reported your account, the report itself is kept so that moderators can still see a pattern of behaviour — but it is stripped of anything identifying you. What remains is the reason the reporter wrote, the date, and any note a moderator added. Your identifier is removed from it.

Ordinary server logs may hold request records for a short period as part of running the service. They are not used to rebuild a deleted account.

This page is for the Kindred Spirits mobile and web app. It is reachable without an account on purpose, so that anybody can find out how to delete theirs.
